## 1. Reddit — r/resumes (huge, exactly this audience)

> **Title:** I built a free ATS checker that shows the exact keywords you're missing — with evidence from your own CV, no signup
>
> Hey! I kept seeing people get auto-rejected and wonder why, so I built a small free tool called CV Lens.
>
> You paste your CV (or drop the PDF) + paste the job posting, and it gives you:
> - A match score + subscores (must-haves, keywords, experience, education, ATS formatting)
> - A keyword table — found / partial / missing — with a quote from YOUR CV as evidence, so you know it's not making things up
> - Where each keyword lives (in your Experience section vs just your skills list — ATS treats these differently)
> - Rule-based formatting checks (dates gaps, action verbs, multi-column layouts, images inside PDFs…)
> - AI rewrites of your weakest bullets (clearly marked, never invents facts)
>
> Everything runs in your browser except the AI call, CVs are never stored or logged, and it works in English, French and Arabic.
>
> No signup, no paywall, no "unlock full report for $19". It's free.
>
> Would love feedback — especially if it flags something wrong, I want the evidence-based approach to stay honest.

*(r/resumes rule of thumb: engage with comments, don't repost the same text in many subreddits the same day.)*
